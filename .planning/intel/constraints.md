### C-33 — State marks: a closed set of seven shapes, never hue alone
- source: EXPERIENCE.md §State Patterns; UX-DR-71..73 · type: schema

### C-36 — Every conflict and reject code, with its sentence and its actions
- source: EXPERIENCE.md §Every conflict and reject code; UX-DR-166..168 · type: api-contract
- A conflict row carries the filled diamond and `--dk-warn`; a reject row carries the hollow square with a diagonal rule, `--dk-crit-edge` block, `--dk-crit` word. A code is one or the other and never both. **[seed]** = quoted verbatim from the seed and never rewritten; **[written here]** = authored in EXPERIENCE.md.
- `order_not_found` — [seed, persona-switch case] the card is the `account_mismatch` card; [written here, otherwise] "This order is not on your card. Nothing was bound." — Discard · Sync as {original persona}, or Discard alone.
- `order_closed` — [seed + written here] "The order was closed before this arrived. **Nothing was bound.**" — **Discard only.**
- `asset_not_in_order` — [written here] "That asset is not on this work order. Nothing was bound. Discard this and capture the assets on your card." — **Discard only** (no *Report something else* here: FR-R1 forbids reaching a referral from this refusal).
- `account_mismatch` — [seed] the queue is carried under a different acting account — **Discard** · **Sync as S. Mabaso** (the original persona, named).
- `proposal_superseded` — [written here] "A newer verification replaced this proposal before your decision arrived. Nothing was bound." — **Re-decide**.
- `already_recorded_differently` — [written here] "This decision was already recorded, and it was recorded differently. Nothing was changed." — Discard · Re-decide.
- `clock_skew` — [seed] "This phone's clock disagrees with the server by 3 h 12 m. Check the phone's time and decide again." — Discard · Decide again.
- `store_evicted` *(reject)* — [seed] "The demo server's memory store made room and this proposal was dropped — re-verify the asset." — **Re-verify**.
- `bad_shape` *(reject)* — [written here] "The server could not read this item — a field was missing or malformed, and nothing was bound. Capture it again." — Discard · Capture again.
- `media_too_large` *(reject)* — [written here] "This photograph is larger than the demo server accepts, so nothing was bound. The demo server caps the size it will store — retake it and a fresh frame is sent." — Discard · Retake. **The act is offered; the result is not promised.** Nothing in this table forecasts.
- `unknown_kind` *(reject)* — [written here] "The server does not recognise what this item is — an older preview queued it and a newer one is running. Reload, then capture it again." — Discard · Reload and capture again.
- `unknown_proposal` *(reject)* — [written here] "The server cannot derive this proposal from any record it holds, so your decision bound nothing. Re-verify the asset and decide again." — **Re-verify**. Names the record, never the instance; no "server restarted" wording.
- Two deliberate non-conflicts: a retry after a timeout returns `duplicate` and is treated as recorded (no card, no message); a decision arriving at an instance that never issued the proposal is **recorded** (ids are deterministic) and no "server restarted" message exists or may be added.

### C-58 — SPEC.md constraints (the fourteen)
- source: SPEC.md §Constraints · type: protocol
1. **No model runs anywhere.** Verification/proposal module declares exactly `(assetId, fixtureSet)`; named-package ban on vision, OCR, inference, speech-recognition, speaker-identification, voice-print, diarisation. Rules out reading the photograph to "look real", any confidence value, OCR of the plate.
2. **The work order is the only authorisation, decided in one accessor** taking the session-derived account; no RBAC tier read; no framework middleware; no Routing Middleware. Rules out a second gate, role-based access, two not-found producers.
3. **Only a named human asserts, and no path creates a finding.** No type, state, route or configuration value produces one; no closed-set member means *the record states this*; a flag is never promoted. Rules out an auto-apply setting, a "high-confidence" bypass, any grade asserting a quotation.
4. **Attribution has exactly one producer.** Every actor field from the session-derived account; no route schema contains one; server-derived facts enumerate their inputs and take none from the body. Rules out client-supplied identity and a client counter feeding a derived field.
5. **The governed sentences are a fixed register, only ever added to or strengthened.** A sentence renders in full or not at all; the ribbon stays in flow. Rules out a sticky ribbon, a "read more", an "authored" chip, trading labelling against polish.
6. **A platform claim is a runtime result.** Storage state, quota, audio format, connectivity, update availability, installed-vs-browser all rendered from the runtime; a documented behaviour with no probe (WebKit's seven-day window) only cited and dated. Rules out "Safari does not support X" from a version table.
7. **Nothing is verified, observed or resolved on the device.** The register is server-only and reaches no bundle. Rules out offline inference, client-side tag validation/autocomplete.
8. **The server store is memory-only, per instance, and says so.** No cross-instance state; cold start and instance change silent. Rules out a database, a shared cache, a "the server restarted" sentence.
9. **What leaves the device is enumerated:** `{sha256, bytes, mime, duration_ms}` plus a bounded thumbnail, on every capture-bearing route including sync and referral; no client module posts an original or audio.
10. **A record-binding control is 130 CSS px, at most two per row**; 328 px content box at 360 px; laid out to hold at 320 px and 200 % with no two-dimensional scrolling. Rules out a three-up row and recovering space by shortening a sentence.
11. **No screen carries more than one primary record-binding action** — a decision (the glove-and-glare rule), not merely arithmetic. Rules out a checklist over a viewfinder and two ways to commit the same record.
12. **13 px type floor; body and label text 7:1 on the dark ground; no state by hue alone** (fill, border, shape on every state). Rules out the parent's 8.5–11 px chips and any state pair separated by tone.
13. **No claim that any standard requires the gate.** Nothing in API 510/570/653, ASME BPVC V, the OHS Act Pressure Equipment Regulations or the EU AI Act (as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744) requires a named human to accept a machine observation; the claimable form is that every rule already assumes a qualified named person evaluates and none contemplated a machine contributing. Rules out "required by API 510", an insurer-mandated framing, a compliance badge.
14. **Readiness is stated per element, never per product.** TRL 4 claimable on the named enforced seams; the observation component at MLTRL 0–1; the instrumented pilot named as what would earn TRL 5. Rules out a product-level number.
- Plus: **no action in the capture flow is gesture-only, and accept and reject are identical at first contact**; accept's confirm is an in-place label swap; reject stays single-tap. Rules out swipe-to-accept, a confirm sheet, bulk acceptance, any default.
