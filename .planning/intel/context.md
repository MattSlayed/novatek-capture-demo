## Topic 1 — The problem (brief)

- source: brief.md §The problem
- **South African law already requires a record of what is found on this plant, and nothing in the working day is built to make it at the asset.** On a generation plant, the document that closes a job certifies that *"all personnel, tools and debris have been withdrawn and that the plant is in a safe and serviceable condition"* (Generation Plant Safety Regulations 240-150642762 Rev 3, cl. 6.1.14). It has no field for what the artisan saw while the machine was open. Nobody may refuse to close a job because nothing was recorded about the asset; so the observation is made, at the asset, by a person with a name, and it survives as speech.
- **The contractor keeps nothing.** The client's planning department captures the work into the client's ERP; the contractor's touchpoint is an order number for invoicing (Eskom Arnot compressor scope, June 2025; Grootvlei, 2022); what it hands over on completion is a safety file (Eskom Standard 32-136). BRIMIS confirms this as its own position, and that its job cards are paper.
- **An out-of-scope observation has nowhere to go.** The Pressure Equipment Regulations 2009 run a continuing-offence penalty per day (reg 20(1)) and require a safety-relevant defect to be reported *"forthwith"* (reg 11(3)); an undated observation cannot establish that date for either party.
- **The history that would end both is the thing not being kept.** The same regulations permit a risk-based inspection regime built on the user's own inspection history (reg 12). *"We quote no cost figure, because none can be sourced."*

## Topic 2 — The solution and what makes it different (brief)

- source: brief.md §The solution; §What makes this different
- Identity comes from the order; nothing is typed. An observation accepted against an asset on that order becomes binding record under that artisan's name, alongside what they were shown when they decided. **No code path writes a finding.**
- Referrals carry what the order does not: deliberately weaker than a proposal, *"entering no gate and binding to nothing"* — dated, attributed, routed to whoever owns that asset's work. *(Wording predates FR-R7 "a referral proposes work"; brief-update candidate — PRD §8.1 Q3.)*
- Accountability runs by retrievable attribution rather than second signature: a supervisor sees who accepted what, when, and what they were shown. This extends the field technician's role from upload-only to *may assert an observation against an asset in a work order assigned to them*.
- It works with no signal, and nothing is decided on the phone; a queued decision is a claim to be re-validated, never a write to be replayed.
- **Not the feature set.** Offline capture is table stakes; camera-to-asset-record has shipped commercially since November 2025; at least two products publish an accept-or-reject stance. What is different: **the gate is an invariant, not a setting** (comparable products are default-in and configurable to auto-apply; Capture is default-out and non-configurable, checkable by inspection); **the proposal is server-derived**; **rejections are record** (no vendor documentation found describing rejected proposals kept as durable record — absence of evidence, claimed only as checked); **the record accrues to the contractor** under the artisan's name (why the buyer is BRIMIS).
- **On regulation, precisely:** in API 510/570/653, ASME BPVC V, the OHS Act Pressure Equipment Regulations, and the EU AI Act as amended by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, nothing requires a named human to accept a machine-generated observation; what is true is stronger — every rule already assumes a qualified named person makes the evaluation, and none contemplated a machine contributing.

## Topic 3 — Readers, success criteria, scope, the ask (brief)

- source: brief.md §Who this serves; §Success criteria; §Scope; §What we need from you; §Vision
- Readers: the artisan (primary; *"a time-served artisan converting plant knowledge into a durable record while remaining employed"*; gloves, glare, one hand) · BRIMIS (the buyer; the durable per-asset engineering record it does not keep) · the site supervisor (retrieval rather than a queue; NOVACORE-side, not a Capture user) · the adjudicator or investigator (origin, integrity and author of a record — ECT Act 25 of 2002 s 15(2)).
- Success criteria (procedural; no first-party figures): (1) No path writes a finding, demonstrable by inspection of the built system. (2) Every bound observation is retrievable by work order with author, timestamp and what the author was shown. (3) Out-of-scope observations exist as record — a count of referrals, starting at zero. (4) At twelve months in a pilot: a per-asset inspection history for a named set of assets, in a form a risk-based inspection case could be built on. (5) *For the preview:* it survives a hostile reviewer — nothing claimed as real that is not enforced server-side. *"A demo a reviewer can disprove with developer tools is worth less than no demo."*
- Scope — **In, first version:** work-order identity with server-side filtering; capture of photographs and spoken notes; the accept/reject gate with retained rejections and server-side attribution; referrals; automatic hours accrual; offline capture, queueing and reconciliation with surfaced conflicts; the record handed to the companion desk-side surface. **Out of the product:** live sensor/SCADA/IoT; modelling of process, physics or layout; scenario planning; on-device inference of any kind. **Out of the first version:** transcription; native applications.
- The preview: *Enforced server-side:* the work-order seam, the accept/reject gate, attribution, offline reconciliation. *Staged from fixtures and labelled:* the visual match and the observations; no model runs. *Specified and newly added:* referrals, as a ninth phase. *Not exercised:* redaction (in the product, in-region masking before any storage outside quarantine).
- The ask: funding to finish the preview and run an instrumented pilot at the Middelburg workshop against a named set of assets; this build sits in a cost bundle disjoint from the companion surface's. Open from the user: devices and connectivity at BRIMIS and client sites; whether work orders are assigned to a named artisan or a team in the client ERP; workshop-only vs client-site capture (legal exposure); what an accepted observation becomes downstream (deviation, NCR, ERP line, evidence at rest). A further fourteen open questions with sources are in the brief addendum §4 (not ingested — see docs/ingest.yaml).
- Vision: the plant's record becomes something made where the work happens, by the person who did it, under their name; the contractor accumulates a per-asset engineering history outliving any contract and any artisan's memory. *"Building it honestly is the whole product."*

## Topic 4 — Decisions taken with the user (seed; "do not relitigate")

- source: CAPTURE-PLAN-SEED.md §Decisions taken with the user
- **Unit verification:** Authored visual match. The artisan photographs the asset; the app shows a staged "matched to record" result from fixtures, labelled *"authored for this preview — no model ran"*. No QR scan, no vision model.
- **Observations:** Authored proposals from the synthetic asset record (a valve with a seal-weep history yields a "weeping gland" proposal), labelled *"authored — no model observed this photograph"*. The accept/reject gate and never-auto-binding are **real** (server-side, person-attributed, rejections retained).
- **Who accepts:** The artisan, on the phone, at the asset. One persona at a time. A "what Walk receives" view shows the payload.
- **Repo & deploy:** GitHub public `MattSlayed/novatek-capture-demo` → Vercel. Camera/microphone Permissions-Policy relaxed deliberately (ipv-demo's `vercel.json` denies both).
- **Referrals — *report something else*:** decided 31 Aug 2026, specified through the PRD (§4.5, FR-R1–R11) and the UX spine. An artisan on an open order can report something on an asset he was not sent for, without leaving the order and without provoking the FR-18 refusal: photograph the plate, then type the tag as read, then capture and speak. The server resolves the tag against the register, attaches a `Flag` if it resolves, and hands the referral onward as a **proposal that work be raised** — this system creates no work order and amends none. A referral carries **no proposals and no accept/reject step**. An unresolved tag is retained, never discarded.

- **`POST /api/referrals` breaches the uniform-404 discipline deliberately, and it is the only route that does.** Bounded to **existence alone**; a tag belonging to another artisan's order returns byte-for-byte what an unassigned tag returns — asserted by a negative test. Resolution is server-side only. The order id in the body is still checked with the ordinary uniform `not_found`. *"Everything an artisan learns here he could have learnt by walking to the plate."*
- `lib/reconcile/apply.ts` is the single implementation behind open/close/verify/captures/decisions/referrals **and** sync. Order of checks, fixed: session → ownership (`order_not_found` / `unknown_proposal`) → idempotency → shape → state. Idempotency: `seen: Map<"${account_id}:${client_id}",{payload_hash,result}>` keyed **per account**; same key + same hash → `duplicate`; same key + different hash → `conflict already_recorded_differently`. `validate.ts` enforces UUID shape on every `client_id`. *(AD-9 fixes the canonicaliser and hash domain.)*
- **Memory store caps** (`store/memory.ts`), seed values *(now owned by `lib/limits`)*: per account 200 captures, 200 decisions, 20 clock segments, 500 `seen` entries; global 5 000 objects, oldest-first eviction **within the offending account first**; TTL 6 h; `BOOT_ID` per instance. ## Topic 10 — Offline and sync semantics (seed; authoritative table; sentences quoted by EXPERIENCE.md)

- source: CAPTURE-PLAN-SEED.md §Offline and sync semantics
- **Online** = `navigator.onLine && !switchOn && probeOk`; probe = `HEAD /api/health` (`cache:"no-store"`) on mount, on `online` event, every 20 s *(interval and staleness → `lib/limits`)*. The "Work without signal" switch makes `apiFetch` throw `OfflineByChoice` before any request; label: *"Work without signal — a preview switch that blocks this app's own requests. Real airplane mode works too."*
- **Queue**: every write goes `enqueue(item)` → `flush()`; online, flush runs immediately. Batched in `created_at` order. Network failure → `queued`, `attempts++`, backoff `min(60s, 2s·2^attempts)`. `sending` reverts to `queued` on reload if the tab died mid-flight.

| Situation | Server outcome | UI says |
|---|---|---|
| Open order offline | recorded as a `device_reconciled` segment, `opened_at` clamped to ≥ session `issued_at` and ≥ last server contact; already open → `duplicate` | "Clock started on this phone at 14:02 (device clock). Reconciled when you reconnect." Walk and the hours screen show the segment's `source`. |
| Verify offline | local `pending`; on sync `server.verification` + `server.proposals` | "Without signal, nothing is verified or observed on this phone. Queued as a draft…" → "3 observations arrived for 20HAD10AA601 — review them." |

| Referral raised offline | queued as `resolution: "pending"`; on sync the server resolves the tag, attaches the flag if it resolves and produces the work proposal. | Persona switched with items queued | `account_mismatch` / `order_not_found` | ConflictCard: **Discard** or **Sync as S. Mabaso**. The `orders` IDB store is namespaced by `account_id` and foreign entries are purged at the gate; queued items are kept (they carry `claimed_account_id`) |

| Decision reaches an instance that never issued the proposal | **Recorded** — proposal ids are deterministic. No "server restarted" message exists for this case. | chip "recorded" |
| Proposal genuinely gone (TTL, or evicted under caps) | `rejected store_evicted` | "The demo server's memory store made room and this proposal was dropped — re-verify the asset." **Re-verify** |

| 401 during flush (session expired mid-shift) | — | every item stays `queued`; the app routes to the gate to re-mint the **same** persona; nothing is discarded |

## Topic 11 — Camera, voice notes, storage (seed; mechanism notes)

- source: CAPTURE-PLAN-SEED.md §Camera and voice notes
- **Secure context**: `getUserMedia`, `crypto.subtle`, `crypto.randomUUID`, `navigator.serviceWorker`, `navigator.storage` are `[SecureContext]`-gated — a phone on a LAN `http://192.168…` gets none. Two phone-testing paths: **Vercel preview deployments** (primary; Deployment Protection off) and **`next dev --experimental-https`** (phone must trust the mkcert root CA; `allowedDevOrigins` lists the LAN origin).
- **Permissions-Policy** must be `camera=(self), microphone=(self)` (ipv-demo's `camera=()` would make `getUserMedia` reject regardless of the user's answer); `scripts/check-headers.mjs` enforces it. The desktop frame renders inline, not in an `<iframe>`.
- **Photo**: `getUserMedia({ video: { facingMode: { ideal: "environment" }, width: { ideal: 1920 } } })` → `<video autoPlay muted playsInline>` with explicit `play()` after a gesture; shutter reads `videoWidth/videoHeight` at capture time; canvas ≤ 1600 px long edge → `toBlob("image/jpeg", 0.82)`. Fallback `<input type="file" accept="image/*" capture="environment">` is **first-class**; decode with `createImageBitmap(file)` (the `imageOrientation: "from-image"` option throws on engines predating the rename — wrap in try/catch). Canvas re-encode strips EXIF — assert in the harness. Thumbnail 320 px JPEG 0.6, **40 KB client target / 64 KB server hard cap, both in base64-encoded bytes** *(→ `lib/limits`)*. SHA-256 over the **full** blob via `crypto.subtle`.
- **iOS media lifecycle**: one active capture per page — **stop camera tracks before starting the recorder and vice versa**; tracks mute on backgrounding and do not always resume — **re-acquire on `visibilitychange`**; a standalone app can be reloaded from scratch when foregrounded — screen/order/asset live in the URL, pending-capture intent in IDB `kv`.
- **React 19 strict effects** double-run in dev: guard `getUserMedia` with a ref; cleanup must `track.stop()` every track.
- **Voice**: `new MediaRecorder(stream, { audioBitsPerSecond: 32000, mimeType })` with `mimeType` from `isTypeSupported` in order `audio/webm;codecs=opus` → `audio/webm` → `audio/mp4` (iOS Safari: AAC/MP4 only) → `audio/ogg;codecs=opus`; if every probe is false, **construct with no `mimeType` and read `recorder.mimeType` afterwards**; only if construction throws, show "voice notes are not available in this browser" — `<input accept="audio/*" capture>` is not presented as a path. `start(1000)` timeslice so partial data survives a killed tab. Measure `duration_ms` by wall clock (WebM blobs report `Infinity`). Hard cap 60 s *(→ `lib/limits`)*. Only `{ sha256, bytes, mime, duration_ms }` leaves the phone.
- **Storage**: IDB `media` store `{ id, blob, thumb, mime, bytes, sha256 }`; `navigator.storage?.persist?.()` feature-detected and caught; the Sync screen reports the tri-state *persistent / best-effort / not supported on this browser*; `storage.estimate()` alongside. The seed's parenthetical "Safari has no `persist()`" **[SUPERSEDED → AD-12: the tri-state is runtime-derived from `persist()`/`persisted()`; no browser-name assumption may select a wording].** WebKit's ITP cap deletes all script-writable storage — IDB, Cache API **and the SW registration** — after **7 days of Safari use without visiting the site**; a home-screen install is effectively exempt. The Sync screen says exactly that *(a cited, dated policy claim under AD-12's second clause)*.
- **Budget**: photo 250–600 KB on device (up to ~800 KB), ≤ 40 KB on wire; voice at 32 kbps ≈ 240 KB/min on device, 0 on wire; sync batch ≤ 2.5 MB encoded (Vercel body limit 4.5 MB) *(→ `lib/limits`; outbound budget open in P6)*.

## Topic 12 — Service worker design (seed; binding regardless of the P7 hand-rolled-vs-Serwist choice)

- source: CAPTURE-PLAN-SEED.md §Service worker
- Registered production-only (a SW fights HMR): `register('/sw.js?v=' + NEXT_PUBLIC_BUILD_ID, { scope: "/", updateViaCache: "none" })`. Worker: `SHELL = "cap-shell-" + v`; `STATIC = "cap-static"` (stable across versions).
- **Precache — the shell must be coherent.** On the first visit the page is not yet controlled, so its `/_next/static/*` chunks would never be cached; an airplane-mode reload would serve cached HTML whose scripts 404. So at `install`: fetch `/` (`cache:"reload"`), **parse the HTML for every `/_next/static/…` URL** (script `src`, stylesheet `href`, `modulepreload`, font preloads) and `cache.addAll` them into `STATIC` alongside the HTML in `SHELL`, plus `/manifest.webmanifest` and the icons. `scripts/check-offline.mjs` proves it (reload offline → zero failed subresources).
- **Runtime**: `/api/*` → **never handled** (network only; `check-sw.mjs` greps for the guard) · `mode === "navigate"` → network-first with a **3 s `AbortController` timeout** (a `Promise.race` would let a late response overwrite the coherent precached shell — never cache the navigation response over the precached `/`), fall back to `SHELL`'s `/` · `/_next/static/**` → cache-first into `STATIC` · same-origin image/font/manifest → stale-while-revalidate · else pass through.
- **Activate**: delete `cap-shell-*` ≠ `SHELL`; prune `STATIC` by count (keep newest ~150) — never wholesale; `clients.claim()`.
- **Update**: on `updatefound`, show "A newer preview is ready — reload" **only if `navigator.serviceWorker.controller` was already non-null**; also check `reg.waiting` at page load. Tap → `SKIP_WAITING`; reload on `controllerchange` **only behind a flag set by that tap** (an unconditional reload is the classic first-visit loop and would break "never auto-reload mid-capture").
- **Headers** (`vercel.json`): `/sw.js` → `no-cache, no-store, must-revalidate`; `/manifest.webmanifest` → `max-age=0, must-revalidate`; `/api/(.*)` → `no-store`.
- **Screen switching is request-free — asserted, not assumed.** `check-offline.mjs` asserts zero `_rsc` and zero document requests during offline screen switches; fallback is a `history.state`-only switcher.
- **Manifest**: `id:"/"`, `name:"NOVATEK Capture — designed preview"`, `short_name:"Capture"`, `start_url:"/?source=pwa"`, `display:"standalone"`, `orientation:"portrait"`, colours `#0c1e35`, icons 192/512/512-maskable, via `app/manifest.ts` (no `metadata.manifest` in `layout.tsx` — the file convention already injects the link). `layout.tsx`: `metadata.appleWebApp`, `viewport.viewportFit:"cover"`; CSS uses `env(safe-area-inset-*)`.
