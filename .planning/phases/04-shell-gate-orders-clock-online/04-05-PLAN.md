<objective>
Build the four primitive modules every later screen measures against: the 130 px record
control, the 44 px secondary control, the row shell, and the seven state marks. Nothing in
this phase binds a record, and that is the point — the record control is designed against
DESIGN.md here rather than under capture-flow pressure in Phase 5.

Purpose: D-06 builds all five primitives in this phase (the ribbon already exists from Phase 1)
and D-07 turns their ownership into a build rule. A later screen must consume these modules
rather than re-declare a size, a contrast pair or a mark shape locally. The recorded risk is
real: a contract written against a single consumer can encode the wrong invariant, and Phase 5
is its first real test and may generalise it. That is expected, not a failure.

Output: four components and four CSS modules, each with a single owner for the declaration it
carries. They are not reachable from any screen yet; plans 04-07 to 04-11 consume them.
</objective>
