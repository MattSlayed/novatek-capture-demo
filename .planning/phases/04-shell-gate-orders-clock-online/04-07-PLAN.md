<objective>
Build the gate: the screen any surface shows when there is no session, carrying FR-48a's
disclosure in full on first entry and the short form plus a reopen control on re-entry, above
three live persona doors.

Purpose: FR-48a requires the enforced/authored split stated before any claim is met. The server
store is memory-only and the session cookie is `HttpOnly`, so no server-side mechanism can know
whether this reader has seen the long form — the claim is about a person, and a shared phone, a
cleared browser and a second device are all readers who have not seen it. The gate therefore
defaults to the long form and downgrades only on proof, which is the only direction that cannot
under-disclose.

Output: one `lib/client/` module with its unit test, and the gate surface. The gate is not yet
reachable — plan 04-11's switcher renders it.
</objective>

| the previous account's document → the next account's | `purge()` in the choice handler is the boundary. |
